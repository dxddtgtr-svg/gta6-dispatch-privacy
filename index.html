Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Dispatch · Last updated: November 27, 2026

About this app: GTA 6 Dispatch is an independent, unofficial news app covering Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

1. Information and app features

GTA 6 Dispatch does not require you to create an account, provide a profile, or submit personal information to read its content. The app does not include its own user-posting or messaging feature. News content may be downloaded from a configured feed and cached locally on your device for offline access.

When you use online features, your device must connect to the relevant servers. Those servers and network providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, device/browser user-agent, request details, and connection logs. The exact information and retention period depend on the hosting provider.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising, which includes banner and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as the Android advertising ID or other device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic or ad-performance data, as permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to present and manage consent choices where required. Available consent and privacy controls depend on your region and the Google SDK configuration. Where required, you can reopen them through Settings → Ad privacy options.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising and data.

3. News feed and video

If remote news or video sources are configured, the app requests that content over the internet. The service hosting the feed or video may process your IP address and technical request information to deliver the content, protect the service, and maintain its operation. Content may be cached on your device. The app does not control the privacy practices or retention policies of those independent hosting providers.

4. Local storage

The app may store limited settings, consent-related state, and cached news content locally on your device so features can work correctly. This information remains on your device unless a feature or third-party service described in this policy sends information online.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in advertising, content delivery, hosting, and network operations, under their own terms and privacy policies. Those providers may operate servers outside Morocco or your country of residence.

6. Children

GTA 6 Dispatch is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, please use that provider's available privacy controls.

7. Your choices and data requests

You can manage advertising consent where the app provides the relevant controls, and clear the app's locally stored data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. You may also use the privacy controls provided by Google. Rights and options available to you depend on the laws that apply in your location.

8. Security, retention, and changes

Local app data is retained on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Third-party providers set their own retention and security practices for information they process. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

This policy may be updated if the app's features, SDKs, or service providers change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made. The policy should be reviewed again before each release to ensure it matches the actual app configuration and Google Play Data safety disclosures.
